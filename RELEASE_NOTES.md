## BrightOura v1.3 — black, and a Settings screen

- **Black background**, the usual LightOS panel ground.
- **A seventh screen: Settings.** Swipe to the end for the Mac bridge address, a **Sync now**
  button, the ring's serial / firmware / battery / frames synced, and a way into direct Bluetooth
  pairing — so things can be fixed from the phone without a rebuild.

Note: the ring must be **worn** (awake) for the Mac to sync it — a bonded ring asleep on a desk
does not advertise, so nothing comes through until it is on your finger or on the charger.
