## BrightOura v1.4 — refresh, and smoother screens

- **A refresh button** on the Today screen (top-right) — tap ↻ to re-read the ring from the Mac.
  It shows "UPDATING…" while it works. (Settings still has Sync now too.)
- **Smoother left/right.** A light flick now commits to the next screen instead of needing a full
  drag, neighbouring screens are kept ready so they slide in without a hitch, and a little spacing
  keeps one screen from bleeding into the next mid-swipe.

The ring's syncing well — overnight heart rate, SpO2, temperature and motion all coming through.
